01

Registration

Finding — and claiming — your club's official FTH address

Session 1 · Step 1 of 7

Look Up Your Club & District Numbers

  • FTH asks for your club number and district number during signup.
  • Don't know them? Use Find a Club on toastmasters.org.
  • Search by your club's name to see both numbers on its listing.
  • Keep them handy — you'll enter both in the next step.
Session 1 · Step 2 of 7

Start the Website Request

  • Go to toastmastersclubs.org/welcome — the official FTH signup page.
  • Read the Terms of Use Agreement before continuing.
  • Choose the tab that matches you: Chartered Club, Unchartered Club, or District Website.
  • Most established clubs will choose Chartered Club.
Session 1 · Step 3 of 7

Enter Your Club & District Numbers

  • Type in the district number you looked up.
  • Type in the club number you looked up.
  • Double-check both — this is exactly what generates your website's address.
Session 1 · Step 4 of 7

Confirm Who You Are

  • Enter your full name and email address — you'll type the email twice, to confirm it.
  • State your connection to the club and your authority to request the site (e.g., you're an officer).
  • This step protects the club — random visitors can't just claim a site.
Session 1 · Step 5 of 7

Submit and Wait for Approval

  • Submit the request.
  • FTH emails confirmation links to your club's registered contacts.
  • Someone with authority approves the request — usually within a few days.
  • Once approved, there's no separate "activation" step.
Session 1 · Step 6 of 7

Your Club's Web Address

  • Every chartered club site follows the same pattern: https://[club number].toastmastersclubs.org
  • Example: club #12345 lives at 12345.toastmastersclubs.org
  • District sites follow d[district number].toastmastersdistricts.org
  • Next: read the Quick Start Guide on the FTH support site, then set your admin password.
Session 1 · Step 7 of 7

Already Have a Site? Claim Admin Access Instead

  • Most clubs already have an FTH site from a past officer — you may just need access, not a brand-new site.
  • Go to toastmastersclubs.org/change and submit the "Request Admin Change" form.
  • It asks for your club number, your name, and your email (entered twice).
  • Submitting sends three confirmation emails — to the current admin, the club president, and the primary Toastmasters International contact. Only one has to approve.
  • Before you submit: make sure your club's contact email on Club Central is current, or the confirmation emails may go nowhere.
Session 1 · Heads Up

02

Building the Home Page

Turning your new site into a welcoming front door for guests

Session 2 · Step 1 of 7

Set Your Basic Settings First

  • Club Name — shows in your site's header/banner.
  • Description — a short pitch about your club; search engines show this, so make it count.
  • Alias / URL — pick a short, memorable web alias if offered.
  • Time Zone — set it correctly so meeting times and daylight saving work.
Session 2 · Step 2 of 7

Find Your Way to the Admin Console

  • Log in, then open the Admin Console — command central for editing your site.
  • Set (or change) your admin password here.
  • Everything in this session happens inside the Admin Console.
Session 2 · Step 3 of 7

Write Your Main Heading

  • The Main Heading is the big line of text just below your site's header.
  • Limit: 100 characters, including any formatting.
  • Use it to say something specific — not just "Welcome."
  • Supports bold, underline, italics, and line breaks.
Session 2 · Step 4 of 7

Replace the Stock Photo

  • New sites start with a generic placeholder photo — swap it for a real club photo.
  • Pick an image that shows people, energy, or your meeting space.
  • Less text, more visual appeal — guests decide fast.
Session 2 · Step 5 of 7

Get Comfortable with the Editor

  • FTH's built-in editor works like a simple word processor — no HTML needed.
  • Click into any text area and start typing.
  • Use the toolbar for bold, links, alignment, and images.
  • Small changes save quickly, so don't be afraid to experiment.
Session 2 · Step 6 of 7

Add & Resize Images

  • Click the image icon in the toolbar, choose Upload, then Browse for your file.
  • Click "Send It To Server," then close the window.
  • To resize, keep the lock icon closed so the image doesn't stretch.
  • Use a % width (like 80%) so images look good on phones too.
Session 2 · Step 7 of 7

Remove Images the Safe Way

  • First: delete the image from the page itself.
  • Then: delete it from the image library.
  • Doing it in the wrong order can slow down — or even crash — your site.
  • When in doubt, remove from the page and stop there.
Session 2 · Heads Up

03

Membership Management

Keeping your roster accurate — one member, or one whole club, at a time

Session 3 · Step 1 of 6

One Thing to Know First

  • FTH does not automatically sync with Toastmasters International.
  • You (or another officer) keep the member list current by hand.
  • Good news: you can bulk-import your whole roster instead of typing each member.
Session 3 · Step 2 of 6

Meet the Membership Panel

  • Open it from the admin pull-down menu.
  • Add members one at a time, or use bulk tools for the whole club.
  • Also where you manage guests, prospects, and former members.
Session 3 · Step 3 of 6

Add Several Members at Once with Quick Add

  • Open the Quick Add tab.
  • Type one member per line: name, then a space, then email, then a space, then phone.
  • No spaces inside the phone number.
  • Use an underscore (_) to join name parts that must stay together.
Session 3 · Step 4 of 6

Import Your Roster from Toastmasters International

  • Log in at toastmasters.org, go to Leadership Central → Club Central.
  • Open Club Business → "View/Update/Print my club membership roster."
  • Click Export to CSV to download your official roster.
Session 3 · Step 5 of 6

Clean the File, Then Paste It In

  • In the spreadsheet, hide columns you don't need and remove spaces from phone numbers.
  • Copy the cleaned name/email/phone columns.
  • In FTH, open Quick Add, paste the data, choose "Members" as the group, and Save.
  • Members missing an email get a placeholder automatically.
Session 3 · Step 6 of 6

Keep It Current

  • Move members who've left to Former Members — don't just delete them.
  • Periodically review your guest and prospect lists.
  • Update officer roles after each election, typically every July and January.
Session 3 · Heads Up
FTH4 · Beta — Coming

Membership Panel Becomes the Member Portal

  • Same core job, new name: today's "Membership Panel" is called the Member Portal in FTH4.
  • Same roster import: the Club Central CSV export/import workflow still works the same way.
  • New: members can log in themselves with a magic link or TOTP, manage their own directory listing and profile, and sign up for roles — instead of only officers editing by hand.
04

Agenda Setup

Building the meeting plan your members will actually follow

Session 4 · Step 1 of 7

Who Usually Does This

  • Typically the VP Education manages agendas, though any assigned officer can.
  • Agendas live in the site's Agenda area, separate from the Admin Console.
  • You can build one from a template, or start from scratch.
Session 4 · Step 2 of 7

Create a New Agenda

  • Click the + icon in the agenda toolbar near the top.
  • Choose the meeting date.
  • Pick a template — FTH may ask if you want to match its default start time.
  • If your club rotates locations, select a Meeting Venue too.
Session 4 · Step 3 of 7

Starting From Scratch? One Rule

  • Add at least one item under Meeting Agenda Items before you close the window.
  • Skip this and the blank agenda disappears entirely.
  • Templates don't have this risk — they come pre-filled.
Session 4 · Step 4 of 7

Find Your Way Around the Editor

  • Click the pencil icon to open editing.
  • Three tabs: Agenda Setup, Meeting Description, and Meeting Agenda Items.
  • Meeting Agenda Items is where roles and speakers get assigned.
Session 4 · Step 5 of 7

Assign Members to Roles

  • Pick a name from each role's pull-down menu.
  • Members marked unavailable appear greyed-out and can't be picked.
  • Leave a role open by choosing "(unassigned)."
  • The count in brackets shows how many times that member has done the role before.
Session 4 · Step 6 of 7

Fine-Tune Any Role

  • Click the pencil icon next to a role's time slot.
  • Add description text guests and new members will see.
  • Set the role's duration in minutes from the dropdown.
  • A 0-minute role simply shows no time slot.
Session 4 · Step 7 of 7

Reorder, Add, or Remove Roles

  • Drag a role by its row to move it up or down.
  • Click the trash icon to delete a role.
  • Fill in the form at the bottom to add a brand-new role.
  • Save after every change — and share the finished agenda by printing or emailing it.
Session 4 · Heads Up
FTH4 · Beta — Coming

  • Coming in v1, not at launch: Pathways tracking per member, a speech/attendance log, and first-class Zoom/Meet/Teams hybrid meeting links built into the agenda.

Build Your FTH3 Site Now — Don't Wait for FTH4

  • FTH4 has no confirmed release date. It's a volunteer beta, and the stewards of toastmastersclubs.org / toastmastersdistricts.org haven't confirmed they're adopting it yet — "wait for it" could mean an unknown length of time.
  • Your club needs a working website today. Meetings, membership, and guest outreach don't pause for a platform rebuild.
  • Here's the good news: migration is designed to be painless. FTH4 keeps the exact same domain and the same TI-verification signup gate on purpose — so what you build on FTH3 today isn't wasted work. It's the on-ramp, not a dead end.
